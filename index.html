HSE Reminder

We're here to have fun, but safety is still the priority. A few reminders before we hit the sand.

Before you come

Not a confident swimmer, or have a health condition we should know about? Tell your team lead before we go, so we can look out for you.

While you are there

  • Wash and sanitise your hands often.
  • Cover your mouth when you cough or sneeze.
  • Contactless greetings work great.
  • Swim between the flags and mind the current. If in doubt, sit that dip out.
  • Sun's strong out here: reapply sunscreen every couple of hours and keep the water topped up.
  • Thunder or lightning nearby? Head indoors with everyone else until it clears.
  • Spot a jellyfish or anything else in the water you don't recognise? Give it space and flag a lifeguard.

Good to know

  • The committee photographs throughout, with a group photo at the end of each event.
  • Feel free to bring your own prayer mat and telekung.
